FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

A credit score is a 3-digit number (300-900) that represents your creditworthiness. It's based on your credit history, repayment behavior, and existing debts. A higher score improves your chances of loan approval and gets you better interest rates. Scores above 750 are considered excellent.

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is calculated using the formula: EMI = [P x R x (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N-1], where P is the loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate, and N is the number of monthly installments. Use our EMI calculator above to get instant calculations.

Typically, you need: identity proof (Aadhaar/PAN), address proof, income documents (salary slips/IT returns), bank statements of last 3-6 months, and passport-size photographs. Requirements may vary by bank and loan type.

Yes, but options may be limited. Some NBFCs and banks offer loans to individuals with scores as low as 550, but at higher interest rates. Improving your credit score before applying can help you get better terms.

Fixed rates remain constant throughout the loan tenure, providing predictable EMIs. Floating rates change with market conditions, which can lower your EMI when rates drop but increase them when rates rise. Floating rates are usually lower initially.

Improve your credit score by paying bills on time, reduce existing debt, maintain a stable income, choose a longer tenure to reduce EMI burden, and add a co-applicant with good credit to strengthen your application.
